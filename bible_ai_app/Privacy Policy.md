# Privacy Policy - Open Shema

Last updated: September 2026

Open Shema is an open-source, offline-first desktop application.

1. Data Collection: Open Shema does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, identifiers, or telemetry to external servers.
2. Local Storage: User notes, highlights, and local databases remain strictly stored on the user's local device.
3. Network Usage: Network requests are limited to downloading study resources explicitly requested by the user.
